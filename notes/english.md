- Relaxed, friendly tone; humour works ("Experience is NONE").

- Conditionals are correct: "if they do not extend our contracts, I will have 6h per day".

## Recurring mistakes (focus list)
Ordered by how much they matter. Remove an item once it stops appearing.

1. **Present perfect for "since then until now"** — "last 6 months I'm developer" → "for the last 6 months I've been a developer"; "it's like that last 7 years" → "it's been like that for the last 7 years".
2. **Articles (a / an / the)** — "I'm Angular developer" → "I'm an Angular developer"; "create learning project" → "create the learning project".
3. **Prepositions** — "on proper way" → "in the proper way"; "prior 2016" → "before 2016" / "prior to 2016"; "last 6 months" → "for the last 6 months".
4. **Verb patterns** — "practicing to speak" → "practicing speaking" (-ing after practice, enjoy, avoid, finish).
5. **Collocations** — "do a commit" → "make a commit"; "do research" (not "do a research").
6. **Extra object** — "stuff I will never use it" → "stuff I will never use".
