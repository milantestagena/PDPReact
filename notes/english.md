## Strengths
- Gets the message across every time; the meaning is never unclear.
- Relaxed, friendly tone; humour works ("Experience is NONE").
- Good conversational phrases: "to be honest", "I trust you to find the balance", "wait".
- Conditionals are correct: "if they do not extend our contracts, I will have 6h per day".

## Style choices (not mistakes — don't correct)
- **Questions by intonation** ("This is for testing only?") instead of inversion — a deliberate choice, fine in speech for yes/no questions.
  Still correct it in wh-questions, where intonation is not enough: "Where it is?" → "Where **is** it?"

## Recurring mistakes (focus list)
Ordered by how much they matter. Remove an item once it stops appearing.

1. **Articles (a / an / the)** — the most frequent one (6+ times in A1). "I'm Angular developer" → "I'm an Angular developer"; "Browser sees" → "The browser sees"; "second part" / "second one" → "**the** second part" (ordinals almost always take *the*); "duplicate part in brackets" → "the part".
2. **Perfect tenses for "before / until then"** — "last 6 months I'm developer" → "for the last 6 months I've been a developer"; past perfect (A1, twice): "I was afraid vibe coding killed my brain" → "had killed"; "I knew we didn't learn it yet" → "hadn't learned".
3. **Verb form after did / didn't** (new in A1) — "I didn't linked" → "I didn't link" (base form after *did*).
4. **Prepositions** — "on proper way" → "in the proper way"; "prior 2016" → "before 2016"; "compiled to" → "compiled into"; "translate to" → "translate into"; "instead S-V inversion" → "instead of".
5. **Collocations / calques from Serbian** — "this has sense" → "this makes sense" ("ima smisla"); "do a commit" → "make a commit"; "my miss" → "my mistake / my bad".
6. **Subject dropped in speech** — "tested." → "I tested it."; "rendered well" → "it rendered fine".
7. **Verb patterns** — "practicing to speak" → "practicing speaking" (-ing after practice, enjoy, avoid, finish).
8. **Extra object** — "stuff I will never use it" → "stuff I will never use".

## Wins
- Corrects himself mid-conversation ("will" → "would", "sens" → "sense"). Good habit.
- "X is there to…", "I understand what you're saying, but…", "in general, …" — natural, interview-ready phrases.
