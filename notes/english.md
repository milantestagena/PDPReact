## Strengths
- Gets the message across every time; the meaning is never unclear.
- Relaxed, friendly tone; humour works ("Experience is NONE").
- Good conversational phrases: "to be honest", "I trust you to find the balance", "wait".
- Conditionals are correct: "if they do not extend our contracts, I will have 6h per day".

## Style choices (not mistakes — don't correct)
- **Questions by intonation** ("This is for testing only?") instead of inversion — a deliberate choice, fine in speech for yes/no questions.
  Still correct it in wh-questions, where intonation is not enough: "Where it is?" → "Where **is** it?"

## Recurring mistakes (focus list)
Ordered by how much they matter. Remove an item once it stops appearing.

1. **Articles (a / an / the)** — the most frequent one (6+ times in A1). "I'm Angular developer" → "I'm an Angular developer"; "Browser sees" → "The browser sees"; "second part" / "second one" → "**the** second part" (ordinals almost always take *the*); "duplicate part in brackets" → "the part". A2 (8+ times): "I missed last part" → "the last part"; "returns the object" → "an object" (first mention); "costs little bit more" → "a little bit"; "in Terminal" → "in the terminal" (self-corrected!). A3: "that's different question" → "a different question"; "because of kid" → "my kid"; "first element" → "the first element"; "in right tree" → "the right tree".
2. **Perfect tenses for "before / until then"** — "last 6 months I'm developer" → "for the last 6 months I've been a developer"; past perfect (A1, twice): "I was afraid vibe coding killed my brain" → "had killed"; "I knew we didn't learn it yet" → "hadn't learned".
3. **Verb form after did / didn't / will** (new in A1) — "I didn't linked" → "I didn't link" (base form after *did*).
   A2: "it will stays" → "it will stay"; "what will happens" → "what will happen" (base form after *will* too).
4. **Prepositions** — "on proper way" → "in the proper way"; "prior 2016" → "before 2016"; "compiled to" → "compiled into"; "translate to" → "translate into"; "instead S-V inversion" → "instead of". A2: "on this way" → "put it this way" (again!); "depends of it" → "depends on it". A3: "on higher level" / "on that level" → "at a higher level" / "at that level" (twice); "similar as" → "similar to"; "on interview" → "in an interview".
5. **Collocations / calques from Serbian** — "this has sense" → "this makes sense" ("ima smisla") — back in A2: "does it have some sense?" → "does it make sense?"; "do a commit" → "make a commit"; "my miss" → "my mistake / my bad"; A2: "why to do this?" → "why do this?" ("zašto da").
6. **Subject dropped in speech** — "tested." → "I tested it."; "rendered well" → "it rendered fine". A2: "forgot the name" → "I forgot the name"; "it's not falsy is simply 0" → "…, it's simply 0". A3: "need to go" → "I need to go"; "should call all" → "it should call all of them".
7. **Third-person -s** (new in A2) — "Render get new element" → "Render **gets** new elements"; "Commit phase map" → "The commit phase **maps**".
8. **Past tense in stories** (new in A2) — "I write the Math.random part before I realize" → "I wrote … before I realized" (both verbs past).
9. **good vs well** (new in A2) — "index works good" → "works well" (*well* describes a verb).
10. **Verb patterns** — "practicing to speak" → "practicing speaking" (-ing after practice, enjoy, avoid, finish).
11. **Extra object** — "stuff I will never use it" → "stuff I will never use".
12. **Question forms** (new in A3) — "how to pass className?" → "How do I pass className?"; "why won't allow it" → "why it won't allow it" (normal word order inside a longer sentence).
13. **Word choice** (new in A3) — "order the elements" → "arrange"; "related to WindCard" → "belongs to"; "without watching" → "without running it"; "shown once" → "logged once"; "hide and sick" → "hide and seek" (long "ee" vs short "i").
14. **Adverb position** (new in A3) — "will be rarely used" → "will rarely be used" (after the first helper verb).

## Wins
- Corrects himself mid-conversation ("will" → "would", "sens" → "sense"). Good habit.
- "X is there to…", "I understand what you're saying, but…", "in general, …" — natural, interview-ready phrases.
- A2: "to be on the same page", "no need to check every single one", "I did it on the first attempt", "now I understand what confused me before" — all natural. Fixed "in Terminal" → "in the Terminal" without being asked.
- A3: "won't allow it" (base form after *will*), "A re-renders Slow, B doesn't" (third-person *-s*), "components that don't use them" (first full technical answer with no grammar mistakes). Focus items 3 and 7 had no mistakes this lesson; remove them if that continues.
